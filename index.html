How to use

  1. Write or paste an OpMode in the Code tab (Java, exactly like OnBot Java) and press Build.
  2. Pick the OpMode in the Driver Station, press INIT, then ▶ START. ■ STOP ends it.
  3. Drive with a USB/Bluetooth gamepad (press START+A to make it gamepad1, START+B for gamepad2) or the keyboard.

Saving your work

Programs are kept in this browser's storage: type a name and press Save; reopen them from the Saved programs list. Browser storage is per computer and per browser and can be cleared, so use Download .java to keep a copy (in your team's GitHub repository, a shared drive, or a USB stick) and Open file… to load it again. A downloaded file is a normal OnBot Java file that can be uploaded to a real Control Hub. Alliance, start position, chassis and speed settings are remembered too.

The game in one minute

  • POLLEN (yellow, 2.8 in) and NECTAR (red/blue, 3.62 in) are launched into your alliance's HIVE CELL. The HIVE is bi-stable: put enough in the upward CELL and it TIPS (20 points), dumping everything out and raising the other CELL.
  • The HIVE mouth is 53.5–65.6 in above the tiles and tilted 30°, so shoot from 4–6 ft away with a high arc. Watch the side view.
  • A robot may carry at most 4 elements. It may not touch the other alliance's NECTAR.
  • AUTO is 30 s: LEAVE the start (3), TIP (20 each), PARK in the LOADING ZONE (5). TELEOP is 2:00: more TIPS, then in the last 60 s fill the four FLOWERs (4 in opening, 21.5 in high) — the alliance whose NECTAR is on top owns the FLOWER (2 per element, +5 for NECTAR at the bottom). Elements in your GARDEN score 1 each. PARK again at the end (5).
  • One staged NECTAR is unlocked into the LOADING ZONE per TIP; the rest with 60 s left. Press Load NECTAR to play human player.
  • AprilTags: four 3.25 in tags on the bottom of every CELL (IDs 30–45). They move with the HIVE, so use them for aiming, not for absolute localisation.

Coordinates

Official FTC field frame: origin at the field centre on the tiles, +Z up, +Y from the red wall toward the blue wall, +X toward the audience. Headings are counter-clockwise from +X. The IMU yaw is zero wherever the robot pointed when the OpMode was initialised (or the last resetYaw()).

What the simulator models (and not)

Motors spin up over ~0.1 s and have the goBILDA free speeds; encoders count wheel rotation, so a robot stalled against a wall still counts. Mecanum strafing is 90 % efficient. Launched balls feel drag and gravity; the launcher wheel slows a little each shot. Things not modelled: ball spin, other robots, motor current limits, battery sag beyond a small voltage drop. The mass needed to tip a HIVE is not published; the default is 8 POLLEN-equivalents (a NECTAR counts as 1.65).
